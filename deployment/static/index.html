How it works

Labels are expensive for satellite imagery; unlabeled tiles are not. The model first learns from images alone, then needs only a few labels.

01

Unlabeled tiles

27,000 EuroSAT Sentinel-2 patches, labels discarded.

02

SimCLR pretraining

Two augmented views of a tile are pulled together; other tiles are pushed apart (NT-Xent loss).

03

Fine-tune

A linear head is added and trained on a small labeled subset.

04

Serve

FastAPI + PyTorch (CPU) in a Docker container.

Limitations

  • Trained on 64×64 European Sentinel-2 RGB patches; accuracy will drop on other sensors, resolutions or regions.
  • The test split is random, so neighbouring tiles may appear in both train and test, which can make scores optimistic. A geographic split is the stricter evaluation.
  • Whole-tile classification only: one label per patch, no segmentation or change detection.
  • Predictions are probabilities from a single model and are not calibrated for operational decisions.
